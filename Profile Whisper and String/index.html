Why this exists

Music has the quiet power to stay with us long after the sound has faded. Whisper & String exists to create original music that lingers — soft, atmospheric, and carefully shaped.

How it works

Each track is crafted across a wide range of genres and themes, from classical and jazz to electronic, stories, animals, science, and quiet reflection. The focus is on atmosphere, emotion, and lasting impression.

What you get

  • Original music across multiple genres
  • Themes including animals, energy, meditation, science & AI, atmosphere, and stories
  • Carefully produced tracks made to linger
  • A growing collection available through the Music Store
